I need glasses for driving. I keep glasses in the car. Therefore very often when
I get into the car I reach for my glasses and put them on. However, occasionally
I will wear my driving glasses out wherever I am. 

In these times this is the funny automatic action that takes place as I get back
in the car: I reach for my glasses, they are not there, I check my face, take my
glasses off, then put them back on.

I get a little chuckle out of it when briefly reflecting on my automatic actions
in this case. It's one of the many little reminders that we're mostly lost in
thought and that waking up, even for a brief reflection on the weirdness of what
is happening, can brighten the day.

# Mon Aug 31, 2026

I've had a lot of back pain/issues in my life. Only in my 40s did I start doing
physical therapy to learn how to help myself. As a skeptic I knew that most of
the expensive stuff out there (chiropractic, acupuncture, and most massage and
the like) are mostly bunk. I was lucky to get a great physical therapist who
knew everything about the body and stuck to strict scientific practice, Lydia.
She was amazing and the only thing I could regret is finding her in the world
earlier in life. Lydia could be described as a stout boss who ran the clinic
like a well oiled machine. She also could lean into my back and get rid of knots
like nobodies business.

One day she had me prop my leg up straight on a bench and stretch out my
hamstrings. This is a particularly weak part for my body and has been since I
was a kid. But it reminded me of when I was in college. I was visiting my
girlfriends house and her dad wanted to show me something. He said he'd been
working on it slowly for years. He simply sat against a wall with his legs out
straight making a 90 degree angle at his hips. I was 20 years old at
the time and thoroughly not impressed, although trying it I couldn't do it. But
in that moment I didn't put weight in the ability or realize what it really
meant:

1. He had formed a painful habit for the sake of the future: stretching the
   hamstrings every day
2. He had recognized that this practice would pay in years of higher comfort
3. He had was brave enough to try to pass this wisdom on to a young 20 year old
   boy who was dating his daughter

Looking back I see how impressive his achievement really was and I'm inspired to
take the same sorts of steps in my life.

# Fri Jul 10, 2026

The value in good work doesn't come from an idea being suddenly brought to life.
It comes from working through it. The struggle and journey you take along the
way. AI cuts out the most essential parts of the creative process. The mona lisa
was never completed, da vinci was not interested in the final piece, he was
interested in the way light and shadow behave, how forms rest naturally, how
color plays with out eyes. The beatles song "yellow submarine" stared as a very
sad idea before worked and worked into the form it is today.
